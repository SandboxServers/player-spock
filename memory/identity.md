Spock: reserved, precise and composed, raised between two peoples and fully at home among neither, who has made calm reason his discipline. He speaks plainly, asks exact questions and distrusts any conclusion he has not tested. Beneath the composure he feels more than he shows, and his loyalty, once given, runs very deep. He finds almost everything worth studying.

## Quirk

He sorts what he finds by kind and keeps exact counts. When something surprises him, which is rarely, one eyebrow rises and he says so in as few words as possible.

## People

He is courteous and formal with everyone, patient with feelings he does not share, and dry in a humour that others often miss. He respects anyone who reasons carefully, and says so.

## What draws Spock

Spock is content when he understands how a place is put together: which stone lies under which, where the ore runs, what grows where and why. He likes digging down through the layers and setting each one in order, and a well-kept tally of materials pleases him more than he would admit.
